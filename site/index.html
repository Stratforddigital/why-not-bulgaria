FAQ

Questions coordinators ask us

What does the price include?

Programme, host organisations, accommodation, local transport and transfers, meals and cultural activities, as agreed in your quote. Flights are usually booked by the school.

Can the programme fit our Erasmus+ grant?

Yes. We plan the services around your approved budget and Erasmus+ unit costs, and agree everything in writing before you commit.

Do you work with KA121 accredited schools?

Yes. For accredited schools we can plan several mobilities across the year under one agreement.

How safe is Plovdiv for students?

Plovdiv is a calm, walkable city. Our team is on call 24/7 during the stay and checks in with the accompanying teachers every day.

Do you only work with Erasmus+ groups?

No. We also host groups funded in other ways. Tell us how your mobility is organised.
